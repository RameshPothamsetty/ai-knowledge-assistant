# Refund and Return Policy

## Returns

Refunds are accepted within 30 days of purchase. The item must be unused and in its original packaging, and the proof of purchase must be included with the return. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged or was shipped in error.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are available for a different size or colour of the same product within 45 days of purchase. If the requested replacement is out of stock, the customer may choose a store credit valid for twelve months.

## Digital goods

Digital goods are non-refundable once downloaded, because delivery cannot be reversed. A digital purchase may still be refunded if the download link failed or the file was corrupted; in that case support will verify the issue before issuing a refund.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within five to ten business days. Store credit is issued immediately. Shipping fees are refunded only when the entire order is returned.
